
ClueGame - Test Selection Explanation

(Jaylene)

testRoomNames():

We tested several room names from our Minecraft-themed setup file,
including Enchantment Room, Trial Chambers, Stronghold, Ancient City,
and End City. We also tested Walkway and Unused to make sure the program
correctly reads both room and space entries

testBoardDimensions():

We tested that our board contains 22 rows and 23 columns. These values 
match the dimensions of our ClueLayout.csv file and verify that the program
reads the entire layout correctly


(Trisha)
testDoorDirections():
We selected cells (3,5), (5,2), (5,10), and (7,2) because they represent
the four possible doorway directions: LEFT, UP, RIGHT, and DOWN.
We also tested cell (0,0) to make sure a regular room cell is not incorrectly
identified as a doorway
testRoomCenterAndLabel():
We selected cells (2,2) and (2,3) because they represent the center and
label of the Enchantment Room. We verified that each cell has the correct
property and that both cells belong to the correct room. We also checked that
the Enchantment Room stores references to the correct center and label cells
testSecretPassages():
We selected cells (1,1), (2,9), (19,1), and (19,18) because they represent
the secret passages connecting Enchantment Room and Ancient City, as well as
Nether Fortress and End City. We also tested cell (0,0) to verify that a regular
cell does not contain a secret passage
