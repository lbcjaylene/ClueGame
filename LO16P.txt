LO16P Clue Players 1 Participation Activity

1)
There should be 6 player objects. In mind we have Steve, Alex, Creeper, Enderman, Villager, and a Zombie.
Each player should have a name, color, starting location and a hand of cards

2)
Since our Minecraft clue board has 9 rooms, our deck should have 21 cards total so 9 rooms, 6 suspects, and 6 weapons.
Each card should have a name and a type so room, person, or weapon

3)
There should be 3 cards for the solution. 1 room, 1 person, 1 weapon card. The cards should be randomly
selected and kept separate from the cards dealt to the players

4)
For a correct deal:
	- All cards except the 3 solution cards should be dealt to the players
	- Each player should receive the same number of cards
	- The same card should not be given to multiple players
	- The number of cards dealt should be calculated based on the deck size and number of players
	- The cards should be randomly distributed 

Our board application: Assuming we use 6 people and 6 weapons
	- 9 rooms
	- 6 people
	- 6 weapons
	- 21 total cards
	- 3 solution cards
	- 18 cards to deal
	- 3 cards per player
